# Smart Turn (Pipecat) — acoustic end-of-turn model: facts for the gate's third decider

Checked 2026-09-30 on primary pages (✅) or from search summaries of the vendor's blog (◐).

- ✅ **What:** open-source "semantic VAD" that "tells you whether a speaker has finished their turn by analysing the raw waveform, not the transcript" (HF model card). Considers "grammar, tone and pace of speech" (GitHub).
- ✅ **Openness:** BSD-2-Clause; weights on Hugging Face (`pipecat-ai/smart-turn-v3`), training code on GitHub (`pipecat-ai/smart-turn`), train/test datasets on Hugging Face (`pipecat-ai/smart-turn-data-v3.x-train/test`). v3.2 blog: "model weights, training code, and datasets all publicly available."
- ✅ **Architecture/size:** Whisper Tiny encoder + shallow linear classifier, ~8M parameters; ONNX 8 MB (int8, CPU) / 32 MB (fp32, GPU).
- ✅ **Input/output:** up to 8 s of 16 kHz mono audio, "include as much context from the current turn as possible"; outputs the probability the turn is complete. Meant to run **with a VAD** (e.g. Silero): the VAD detects a pause, Smart Turn decides if it's the end.
- ✅ **Latency (v3 blog):** 12.6 ms on AWS c7a.2xlarge CPU, 3.3 ms on an L40S GPU, 59.8 ms on a small c8g.medium CPU (incl. ~3 ms preprocessing).
- ✅ **Accuracy (v3 blog, English):** 94.31% on an open test set (2,846 English samples), 2.64% false positives, 3.06% false negatives. Weaker languages: Vietnamese 81.27%, Bengali 84.10%.
- ✅ **v3.2 (Jan 7, 2026):** short utterances ("yes", "okay") "miscategorized 40% less often" than v3.1; more robust to cafe/office background noise; drop-in replacement for v3.1.
- ◐ **v3.1 blog:** historically "heavily reliant on synthetic data generated by TTS"; adding human audio had "a significant and measurable effect on accuracy" — i.e. synthetic speech lacks natural cues. Implication for us: validate on real voices (own recordings or FDB v1/v1.5 pause data), not Kokoro audio.

Not stated anywhere checked: accuracy on Indian-English accents; behaviour on 1–2 s thinking pauses specifically.
